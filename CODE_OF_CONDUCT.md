# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus on the technical problem, not the person.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Report problems that affect the safety or wellbeing of the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, sexualized language or attention, publishing private information, and any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when this code of conduct is violated. Reports should be made privately through GitHub's security reporting tools or by contacting a project maintainer through the repository's available channels.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where possible.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project or its community.
